Pre-Mortem 
STEP 1 — ASSUME FAILURE.
EVA is dead. Q2 2027. Boschetti is back to pure Italian civil litigation. Revenue: €8K (one partial case). The website still exists but hasn't been updated since October 2026.

STEP 2 — FAILURE MODES.

FM1: Boschetti hit a capacity wall by week 6.
The email campaign to 19K contacts worked — 3-4% response rate = 570-760 inquiries. Each requires a 45-minute scoping call + follow-up memo. At 5-10 mandates, delivery requires 15-25h of legal work each. Boschetti is one person. Top-of-funnel succeeded; conversion-to-delivery pipeline collapsed.
Foreseeable? Entirely. The plan never mapped Boschetti's available hours per week.

FM2: NHR/IFICI SEO window closed faster than expected.
Competitors updated content within 90 days. By November 2026, 4-6 established firms had published comprehensive NHR/IFICI guides. EVA's domain (4 months of authority) ranked briefly. The window was real but narrower than modeled.

FM3: The 19K contact list was stale and miscategorized.
Average contact age: 4-6 years. 41% hard bounce rate. ~1,400 effective opens. ~7 inquiries. The campaign underperformed because list quality was never audited.

STEP 3 — EARLY WARNING SIGNS.
- FM1: Boschetti's calendar fills week 3 post-launch. He starts declining calls. He tells himself "I'll hire someone when revenue starts." He doesn't.
- FM2: Domain Authority at launch is 0. First page rankings don't appear until January 2027 — when the window has narrowed.
- FM3: First test send shows 38% bounce rate. Noted, rationalized, campaign proceeds.

STEP 4 — FATAL ASSUMPTION.
One individual can simultaneously be business developer, marketing executor, lead qualifier, and legal delivery engine. They don't have compatible time demands. Legal delivery is deep, focused work. Lead generation is interruptive, reactive work. One kills the other.

STEP 5 — RETROSPECTIVE ACTION.
Before launching the email campaign: hire one part-time intake coordinator (10h/week at €20/h = €800/month) with authority to qualify leads and schedule calls. Without this, any successful lead generation campaign becomes a liability.

Galileo — 5 Whys + Constraint Mapping

5 Whys 
STEP 1 — 5 WHYS.
Why 1: Does the GTM plan hold? → Because it assumes activities can be run sequentially by one person without load collisions.
Why 2: Why does sequential execution matter? → Legal mandates are not interruptible. Active delivery prevents simultaneous lead qualification + SEO + LinkedIn.
Why 3: Why weren't workload types mapped? → The plan was built around revenue targets and channel activities, not the binding constraint: Boschetti's available hours per week.
Why 4: Why is the binding constraint not the budget? → €10-13K is not the scarce resource. Boschetti's time is. Budget covers tools; delivery requires legal expertise + relationship hours.
Why 5: Why does this create structural failure risk? → 30-50 mandates implies 20-40 active client relationships managed by one person who also handles intake, compliance, partner coordination, and SEO content.

STEP 2 — ROOT PROBLEM.
"Has Boschetti ever mapped his true available capacity, and does the plan's output target fit within it?" The GTM plan optimizes for demand generation without modeling supply constraints.

STEP 3 — CONSTRAINT MAP.
Hard: Boschetti = 1 person ~50-55h/week total. Italian practice has pre-committed hours. GDPR/LGPD compliance for 19K list. Portuguese Bar rules on foreign lawyers. New domain = 0 SEO authority.
Soft (with evidence assessment):
- "19K warm contacts" — ASSUMED warm. GDPR opt-in status unknown. HIGH RISK.
- "Zero competitors on NHR/IFICI" — TRUE as of June 2026, 90-day window at best.
- "Spain GV orphaned traffic" — REAL opportunity. Strongest channel in the plan.
- "6-8 country expansion" — SOFT. No operational basis.
- "€122.5K revenue target" — ASSUMED. No funnel math connecting channel → mandate.

STEP 4 — REFRAME.
Not: "Does this GTM plan hold?"
But: "Given Boschetti's true weekly EVA capacity, what is the maximum mandates deliverable per quarter without breaking quality or Italian practice commitments?"

STEP 5 — RECOMMENDATION.
Run a capacity audit before anything else. If EVA capacity < 15h/week, the 30-50 mandate target is physically impossible and the GTM plan is irrelevant. If < 15h: (a) reduce Italian commitments now, or (b) hire intake coordinator pre-September, or (c) restructure as referral network model.

Machiavelli — Steelman-then-Attack
Status: Parzialmente Superato · Steelman held for 70%+ commitment scenario · Attack landed on Italian practice unpredictability

Steelman → Attack 
PHASE 1 — STEELMAN.
The GTM plan is not reckless — it is sequenced. Email campaign hits September before active delivery ramps. LinkedIn is modest (5-10/week) = brand-building track, not volume. SEO content can be ghostwritten. Portugal pilot contains delivery to one jurisdiction where Ioana handles case work. The Germany organic lead proves deal flow exists without formal GTM.

Strongest version: Boschetti is a 20-year veteran who knows how to run a practice. 30-50 mandates over 12 months = 2.5-4/month — not 50 simultaneous cases. With Ioana absorbing PT delivery, Boschetti's role is intake + oversight + key relationship management. The capacity wall may exist at 50 mandates, not at 8.

On SEO: in high-trust domains (legal), content from a practicing specialist outranks generic content. The domain authority gap may be smaller than feared because the quality signal is genuinely differentiated.

PHASE 2 — ATTACK.
The steelman rests on sequencing — but sequencing only works if October delivery ramp doesn't collide with September inquiry volume. If 19K contacts generate 400-600 inquiries in 3 weeks, the bottleneck appears in week 3 of September — before any revenue arrives. Boschetti enters October already behind.

On SEO quality: NHR/IFICI is not low competition. It attracts Nomad Capitalist, Expat Network, and specialist firms with DA 40-70. A new domain at DA 0 ranks for long-tail variants, not head terms. Year 1 organic traffic from long-tail NHR keywords: likely 200-500 visits/month — not enough for 30+ mandates.

Deepest attack: The plan has no failure mode for the Italian practice. Year-end legal work surge in Italy (October-December) may collide with exactly the post-September campaign conversion window. Italian practice load is not fixed and predictable — it never is.

Verdict: The steelman holds for a scenario where Boschetti is 70%+ committed to EVA. It collapses if he's 40-50% committed. The plan never specifies which it is. That omission is the single most dangerous gap.
