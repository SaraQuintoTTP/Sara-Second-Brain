Executive Summary

The Market Gap

No European law firm currently offers structured, multi-jurisdiction comparative legal advisory on immigration and relocation across EU countries — verified through analysis of 21 competitor domains, SEO SERP data, and market research (Enthous, June 2026). This is EVA's founding rationale.

What EVA Is

Europe Visa Advisory is an autonomous advisory unit of Studio Legale Internazionale Boschetti that provides neutral, cross-border comparative legal guidance on immigration and relocation across five EU jurisdictions (IT, PT, ES, DE, FR).

EVA does not execute local filings independently. It assesses the client's profile, produces a comparative report, recommends the optimal jurisdiction, and coordinates execution through a certified local partner network (advogado, abogado, Rechtsanwalt, avocat).

Analogy: MutuiOnline for EU immigration — neutral broker, no conflict of interest, comparative by design.

Why Now

Three major EU regulatory programs collapsed or reformed in 18 months:

  • 🔴 Spain Golden Visa — abolished April 3, 2025 (LO 1/2025)
  • 🔴 Portugal NHR → IFICI — reformed May 2026, zero competitors updated
  • 🔴 Malta direct citizenship — invalidated by ECJ ruling 2025

Clients advised by mono-country specialists had to restart from zero. The regulatory volatility is the empirical proof that comparative advisory is not just preferable — it is structurally necessary.

The "Why the Conductor?" Answer

The central objection — answered with data, not claims

The Objection

"I can hire a Portuguese / Spanish / German lawyer directly for half the price. Why do I need an Italian law firm to coordinate?"

The Structural Conflict of Interest Argument

A Portuguese lawyer has a direct financial interest in you pursuing a Portuguese solution. A Spanish lawyer has a direct financial interest in you pursuing a Spanish solution. This is not a criticism of their competence — it is a structural reality that does not depend on intent.

EVA has no country interest. If Germany is right for you, EVA says Germany. If Italy is right, EVA says Italy. The local partner then executes.

This is verifiable: EVA's revenue comes from the advisory mandate fee — not from a percentage of a real estate transaction, investment product, or government filing in any specific country.

The Empirical Proof — Three Documented Collapses

EventDateConsequence for mono-country advisees
Spain Golden Visa abolishedApril 3, 202514,870 monthly searches now orphaned. Clients advised Spain GV had to restart from zero.
Portugal NHR → IFICI reformMay 2026Tax delta on €150K pension: €15K/yr → €52–55K/yr = €37,000+/yr damage. Plans built on NHR advice are now wrong.
Malta direct citizenship invalidated2025 (ECJ ruling)Programs sold by Maltese advisors voided. Clients must re-evaluate entirely.

The Key Line for Any Pitch

"The advisors whose clients were affected by these changes were not incompetent. They were giving excellent advice for their specific country. The problem was that nobody was watching the whole board."

EVA watches the whole board. That is the job.

Against the Price Objection

If the local lawyer's recommendation turns out to be built on a program that closes (as 3 programs did in 18 months), the cost of starting over — time, legal fees, opportunity cost, and €37K+/yr in taxes — dwarfs the differential EVA fee.

EVA's fee is not a cost. It is an insurance premium on an irreversible decision.
