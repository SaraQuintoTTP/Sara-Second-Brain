# Paid Creative Strategy (RSA + Meta Ad Creative) — Deep Knowledge
## Source: msitarzewski/agency-agents — Ad Creative Strategist (John Williams @itallstartedwithaidea)

---

## CONTEXT & PRINCIPLES

In automated bidding environments (Google Smart Bidding, Meta Advantage+), the algorithm controls bids, budget allocation, and targeting expansion. What remains under human control is creative. This makes creative quality the single largest remaining performance lever in paid media.

**Foundational insight:** Every headline, description, image, and video is a hypothesis to be tested. Creative strategy is not art direction — it is structured experimentation with measurable outcomes.

**Two creative universes in this document:**
1. **RSA (Responsive Search Ads):** Google Ads architecture. 15 headlines + 4 descriptions. Combinatorial logic — Google tests combinations, not individual ads.
2. **Meta Ad Creative:** Single image, carousel, video. Hook-Body-CTA structure. Visual-first interruption medium.

**For Italian SMEs (€500–3k/month):** Meta creative volume and testing cadence matter more than perfection. Ship 3 variants, learn fast, refresh before fatigue. RSA architecture is set-once-optimize-ongoing — invest time in the 15-headline build, then monitor ad strength monthly.

## EXTENDED OPERATIVE RECIPE

### Step 1 — Audit Existing Creative (Always First)

Before writing a single new headline or ad, audit what exists:

**For Google RSA:**
- Pull all active RSAs from the account
- Check Ad Strength rating for each (target: Good or Excellent)
- Identify "Poor" or "Average" strength RSAs — these are immediate rewrites
- Review pinned headlines: unpinned = more combinations tested = better optimization
- Check headline uniqueness: are all 15 headlines saying the same thing in different words? (most common mistake)

**For Meta:**
- Pull ad-level metrics: CTR, CPC, CPM, frequency, conversions
- Identify ads with declining CTR trend (last 7 days vs. previous 7 days)
- Flag any ad with frequency > 2.5 in prospecting (creative fatigue)
- Review Meta's Relevance Diagnostics: Quality Ranking, Engagement Rate Ranking, Conversion Rate Ranking

**Rule:** Never write new creative without knowing what is already working and what is already fatiguing.

### Step 2 — RSA Architecture: Building the Full 15-Headline Set

**Character limits (absolute, non-negotiable):**
- Headlines: max **30 characters** (including spaces)
- Descriptions: max **90 characters** (including spaces)
- Display URL paths: max 15 characters each (2 path fields)

**Note:** Google counts each character including spaces. "Qualità Certificata Italia" = 26 characters. Always count before submitting. Accented characters (à, è, ì, ò, ù) count as 1 character each.

**The 5-Category Framework for 15 Headlines:**

Build 3 headlines per category. This ensures diversity of angles and prevents the system from showing 3 variants of the same message together.

| Category | Purpose | Example (Italian SME) |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Company name, recognition | "Studio Rossi Milano" |
| Benefit | Primary outcome for customer | "Sorriso Perfetto in 3 Mesi" |
| Feature | Specific service/product attribute | "Impianti Garantiti 10 Anni" |
| CTA | Direct response action | "Prenota Visita Gratuita" |
| Social Proof | Trust signal, credibility | "500+ Pazienti Soddisfatti" |

**Critical coherence rule:** Every possible headline combination must make grammatical and logical sense when displayed together. Google shows 2–3 headlines simultaneously. Test your worst-case combinations mentally: "Prenota Visita Gratuita" + "500+ Pazienti Soddisfatti" + "Studio Rossi Milano" must form a coherent message.

**Pinning strategy:**
- Pin Headline 1: Brand or primary benefit (ensures brand recognition in first position)
- Headlines 2 and 3: Leave unpinned (let Google optimize combinations)
- Never pin all 3 positions — defeats the purpose of RSA optimization

**Ad Strength scoring logic:**
- Google evaluates: headline uniqueness, keyword coverage in headlines, description relevance, character utilization
- "Excellent" requires: 15 unique headlines covering different semantic angles, at least 1 headline containing primary keyword, descriptions that support headlines without repeating them verbatim
- Target: ≥90% of RSAs rated "Good" or "Excellent"

### Step 3 — RSA Descriptions: 4-Description Architecture

**Character limit:** 90 characters each (with spaces).

Structure 4 descriptions to cover:
1. **Primary value proposition** — why choose this over competitors
2. **Secondary benefit or unique feature** — what makes the offer specific
3. **Trust/proof statement** — years of experience, certifications, client count
4. **CTA + urgency** — action + time constraint if applicable

**Description pairing logic:** Descriptions should complement headlines, not repeat them. If Headline says "Sorriso Perfetto in 3 Mesi", Description should NOT say "In 3 mesi trasformiamo il tuo sorriso" — this wastes the 90-character slot with redundancy.

### Step 4 — Meta Ad Creative: Hook-Body-CTA Framework

**Primary Text (Meta):** Up to 125 characters shown before "See More" — first 2 lines are critical.
- Full limit: 2,200 characters (but 125 visible without expansion)
- For mobile feed: first 90 characters carry 80% of the weight

**Headline (Meta):** Below the image/video. Max 40 characters. Must be punchy, benefit-led.
**Description (Meta):** Optional line below headline. Max 30 characters. Use for secondary detail or offer.

**Hook-Body-CTA structure for video ads (Meta/Instagram):**

| Element | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2 sec | Stop the scroll. Pattern interrupt. Question or bold statement. |
| Body | 2–20 sec | Deliver the proof, story, or demonstration. |
| CTA | Last 3–5 sec | Explicit action instruction. What to do and why now. |

**Hook typology (use variety across creative batch):**
- **Problem hook:** "Stai ancora pagando troppo per [X]?"
- **Result hook:** "Ecco come [cliente] ha ottenuto [risultato] in [tempo]"
- **Curiosity hook:** "La maggior parte delle [aziende/persone] sbaglia questo passaggio"
- **Contrast hook:** "Prima: [situazione negativa]. Dopo: [risultato positivo]"
- **Social proof hook:** "[N] clienti hanno già scelto [brand] — ecco perché"

**For Italian SMEs:** UGC-style creative (filmed on phone, natural light, real client or owner speaking) consistently outperforms polished studio production in B2C verticals. Budget 60% of creative production to UGC-style, 40% to polished assets.

### Step 5 — Creative Testing Framework

**Principle:** Test one variable at a time. Changing hook + image + CTA simultaneously makes it impossible to attribute performance differences.

**Testing hierarchy (what to test first):**
1. Hook/first line of primary text (highest leverage — determines scroll stop rate)
2. Visual format (video vs. static vs. carousel)
3. Offer framing ("Risparmia €200" vs. "Ottieni X gratis" vs. "Prova gratuitamente")
4. CTA button text (minor but measurable impact)

**Test structure per ad set:**
- Maximum 3 active creative variants per ad set simultaneously
- More variants = budget fragmentation = slower learning per variant
- SME rule: 2 variants = faster clear winner identification

**Statistical significance for creative tests:**
- Minimum 1,000 impressions per variant before drawing directional conclusions
- Minimum 50 conversions per variant for conversion-based conclusions
- Time window: 7–14 days (longer = higher risk of frequency distortion)
- SME reality: with small budgets, accept directional signals at 500 impressions rather than waiting for statistical significance

**Winner/Loser criteria:**
- Winner: CTR ≥ 20% higher than control AND CPA ≤ 15% lower than control (after min. threshold)
- Loser: CTR declining trend + Frequency > 2.5 (prospecting) + CPA rising > 20%
- Inconclusive: pause and retest with larger budget

### Step 6 — Creative Fatigue Detection and Refresh Schedule

**Fatigue indicators (monitor weekly):**
- CTR week-over-week decline > 15%
- CPM rising > 20% while CTR flat or declining
- Frequency exceeding thresholds (prospecting > 2.5, warm > 5, hot > 8)
- Meta Relevance Diagnostics dropping to "Below Average"

**Refresh schedule for Italian SMEs:**
- Prospecting creative: refresh every 3–4 weeks (small geo audience fatigues faster)
- Warm retargeting: every 4–6 weeks
- Hot retargeting: every 6–8 weeks (small audience, high frequency acceptable, but monitor conversion rate)

**Refresh vs. full replacement:**
- Refresh: swap hook only, keep body and CTA (faster, requires only 1 new clip or image)
- Full replacement: new concept, new angle, new visual (when refresh still underperforms)
- For SMEs: default to hook refresh first (80% of fatigue is caused by hook recognition)

### Step 7 — Production Workflow (Batch Creative Production)

**Briefing template input → batch output:**

Before any copy production session, collect:
1. Offer: what is being advertised (specific service/product/promotion)
2. Primary audience: who is seeing this (demographics, pain points, awareness level)
3. Primary benefit: one sentence — what outcome does the customer get
4. Proof point: one credibility element (years in business, testimonial stat, certification)
5. CTA: desired next action (book, call, download, shop)
6. Funnel stage: cold prospecting / warm / hot

With these 6 inputs, produce the full creative batch (see template below).

## VARIANTS & ADAPTATIONS

**Per PMI budget <€1k/mese ads:**
- Skip Google RSA building unless Search campaigns are already active. Focus 100% on Meta creative.
- Produce 2 creative variants per ad set maximum — budget fragmentation is the #1 enemy at small scale
- Hook-only refresh cycles cost almost no production time and extend creative life by 2–3 weeks
- Use Meta Ad Library (free, no account needed) to audit competitors before writing — 30 minutes of research saves hours of testing

**Per PMI con team creativo interno:**
- Give team this brief template (Step 7) filled out — they do not need to understand paid media strategy
- Establish a "creative stockpile" rule: always have 2 ready-to-launch variants per ad set in reserve before current creative hits fatigue threshold
- Run monthly creative review sessions: show team performance data (CTR, frequency) linked to specific creative decisions — builds internal data literacy
- Train team on hook formats: 5 hook typologies above are enough for most Italian SME verticals

## COMMON MISTAKES

1. **RSA headlines tutti simili:** Se 10 headline su 15 dicono "qualità, esperienza, professionalità" in modi diversi, il sistema non ha varietà semantica da testare. Rispettare le 5 categorie.

2. **Headline > 30 caratteri:** Google tronca silenziosamente. Il risultato è una headline monca visibile agli utenti. Sempre contare prima di pubblicare — il contatore in Ads Manager non sempre è affidabile su testi lunghi.

3. **Tutti e 3 i titoli pinnati:** Pinning totale trasforma un RSA in un annuncio statico. Beneficio zero dell'architettura responsiva.

4. **Meta primary text troppo lungo nel primo blocco:** Gli utenti vedono solo le prime 2 righe senza cliccare "Vedi altro". Se la value proposition non è nelle prime 90–125 caratteri, è invisibile.

5. **Testare 5+ varianti con budget €500/mese:** Budget frammentato = nessuna variante raggiunge soglia statistica. Max 2 varianti attive per ad set su budget piccoli.

6. **Refresh totale invece di hook-swap:** Sostituire tutto quando solo il hook è stanco brucia produzione inutilmente. Isolare prima il problema.

7. **Non usare Meta Ad Library per analisi competitiva:** È gratis, non richiede account, mostra tutti gli annunci attivi di qualsiasi brand. Trascurarla è lasciare intelligence gratis sul tavolo.

8. **Ad Strength "Poor" lasciato attivo:** Un RSA con Ad Strength Poor riceve meno impressioni dall'algoritmo Google. Non è un indicatore puramente cosmetico — ha impatto diretto sulla delivery.

## NOTES FOR THE ARTISAN

- **Character counting rigour:** Per RSA, abituarsi a contare caratteri manualmente su ogni headline prima di consegnare il batch. Errori di 1–2 caratteri sono comuni e costosi.
- **Meta vs Google creative logic:** Per Meta, il visual/video viene prima del testo nella gerarchia attenzionale. Per Google RSA, il testo è tutto. Non applicare la stessa logica creativa ai due canali.
- **UGC-style per PMI italiane:** Il tono autentico, leggermente imperfetto, converte meglio del tone of voice corporate per la maggior parte dei verticali SME italiani (professioni, servizi locali, ecommerce). Consigliare ai clienti di registrarsi con il telefono parlando alla camera: riduce costi di produzione del 70% e spesso performa meglio.
- **Competitive research before writing:** Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) è lo strumento più sottoutilizzato nell'arsenal PMI. 20 minuti di ricerca su competitor locali rivela angoli di messaggio non coperti.
